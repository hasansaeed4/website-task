Course Overview

Systems & Automatic Control (EC217) introduces the fundamental principles of control systems and their real-world applications. The course begins with basic definitions and the need for control, then covers the classification of systems — including open-loop and closed-loop control and their comparison. From there it moves into mathematical modelling of systems using transfer functions, and the analysis of system behaviour through poles, zeros, and the characteristic equation. Students apply these ideas to electrical and mechanical systems to understand their dynamic response.

Topics covered

  • Basic definitions and requirements of control systems
  • Classification of control systems
  • Open-loop vs closed-loop systems (and their comparison)
  • Components of a closed-loop system
  • Transfer functions and their properties
  • Poles, zeros, and the characteristic equation
  • Modelling of electrical and mechanical systems
